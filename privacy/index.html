Privacy Policy — Gaten

Android app, package com.gaten · published by Anurag Raut
Last updated: 23 October 2026

Gaten is an offline Japanese dictionary and study companion for Android. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. It applies to the Gaten Android app only, and it describes what the app actually does — not what it might do.

The short version
  • No account. Gaten has no sign-up and no Gaten login.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • Screenshots and the Japanese text read from them are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded.
  • The network is used for exactly two optional things: downloading an optional OCR model, and talking to WaniKani — and only if you connect your own WaniKani token.
  • We do not sell your data. We have no server that receives it.

What stays on your device

None of the above is sent to us. Gaten has no backend service.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

1. WaniKani — only if you connect your own account

If you paste a WaniKani personal access token into Settings, Gaten sends that token over HTTPS to api.wanikani.com to authenticate you, and then reads your own WaniKani study data from the same API: your level, the reviews due now and over the next 24 hours, the assignment and review-statistics data behind the Analytics screen, and the subject pages for the kanji and radicals you open. Images for radicals that have no character come from cdn.wanikani.com.

  • The token is stored encrypted on your device and is sent to no one except WaniKani.
  • Nothing is fetched in the background. WaniKani pages are cached on your device and read from there offline.
  • Tapping Disconnect deletes the token and the entire cache.
  • WaniKani is operated by Tofugu LLC, not by us, and your use of it is governed by WaniKani's own privacy policy and terms. Gaten is not affiliated with WaniKani and bundles none of its content.

2. The optional OCR model download

If you tap Download on the settings screen — the app shows you the file size first — Gaten fetches the optional manga-ocr model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Only the file request is sent. If you skip it, the app still recognises Japanese text using Google ML Kit, which performs the recognition on your device.

3. Library-level network access

Gaten declares the INTERNET permission. Gaten's own code contacts only the two hosts above. Two of the libraries Gaten bundles — Google ML Kit and ONNX Runtime, which perform the on-device text recognition — declare the same permission and ship dormant diagnostic components for their own telemetry. Gaten's code never calls them, and images and recognised text are processed on your device: Gaten sends them to no one.

Android backup

Android's own backup service may copy app data — your settings and your recent lookup and search history — to your personal Google Drive, if you have device backup switched on. That copy is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by Gaten, and it is not something we can read. Your WaniKani token and its cache are stored in a location Android never backs up, so they are never included, and the optional OCR model is too large to be included.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Accessibility service — optional and off until you enable it in Android's settings. It lets Gaten read the Japanese text on the screen you are looking at, on demand, when you trigger a lookup. It is not used to monitor your activity.
  • Notification listener — Gaten does not read your notifications. It declares this service only because Android gates access to the active media session behind notification access; Gaten uses the media session so it knows when something is playing.
  • Internet — the two optional uses described above.
  • Network state — to warn you before downloading the OCR model over mobile data.
  • AnkiDroid — Gaten can add the words you look up to your AnkiDroid decks. This is on-device communication with AnkiDroid's own database, through AnkiDroid's published API. Nothing goes over the network. AnkiDroid is a separate application by a separate team, with its own privacy policy.

Sharing, retention and deletion

  • We do not collect your data, so there is nothing for us to sell, share, rent or retain.
  • Everything the app stores lives in Gaten's private app storage on your device. Uninstalling Gaten, or using Android's Clear storage for Gaten, deletes all of it.
  • To delete only the WaniKani credential and its cache, use Disconnect in Settings → WaniKani.
  • The data Gaten sends to WaniKani is held by WaniKani under your own WaniKani account, and is deleted through WaniKani, not through Gaten.

Children

Gaten is a general-purpose language tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children.

Security

Screenshots and recognised text are processed in memory on your device. Your WaniKani token is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key that is generated in, and never leaves, the Android Keystore, and it is stored where Android's backup cannot reach it. All network traffic to WaniKani and to Hugging Face uses HTTPS.
