## Romaji probes (`loose` table)

What a romaji query resolves to, through both lookups the app runs. The kana is what the app's converter (`Wanakana.toHiragana`) makes of the query — the same conversions are pinned by `DictionarySearchTest` — and the loose form is `loose()` of it, the key the `loose` index is asked for. The build fails unless every probe returns a row carrying its expected spelling (the headword of a kanji entry, or a reading of a kana-headed one).
