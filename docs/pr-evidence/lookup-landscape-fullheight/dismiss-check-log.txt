Why the earlier "did not dismiss" reading was not a defect
---------------------------------------------------------
The earlier presses were not attributed to the panel's own bounds, so a press that landed inside
the panel (or before a result existed, when no sheet is mounted and a tap on the frozen screen is
an ordinary lasso tap) reads the same as a press that failed. Re-run against the panel geometry
measured from `12`/`14` — portrait panel top y=702, landscape panel x 400..1939 — with a press
inside the panel as the negative control.

| Orientation | Press (px)              | Expected      | Observed                                                            |
|-------------|-------------------------|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| portrait    | inside panel (250,1100) | no dismissal  | no `Overlay window removed`; screen byte-identical (md5 2bd7fdfe…)  |
| portrait    | above panel (540,300)   | dismiss       | `Media resume…` + `Overlay window removed (resumed=true)`            |
| landscape   | inside panel (1700,900) | no dismissal  | 0 `Overlay window removed` lines                                    |
| landscape   | side gutter (150,540)   | dismiss       | `Media resume…` + `Overlay window removed (resumed=true)`            |

The portrait pair is `16-lookup-dismiss-portrait.mp4` (sheet up at ~8 s and ~11 s after the inside
press, app with no overlay from ~15 s); the landscape pass is `17-lookup-dismiss-landscape.mp4`
(sheet up, then the gutter press at ~9 s clears it).
