Why adb for the trigger/tap/stills
  agent-device's snapshot/input path attaches UiAutomation to the device, and an attached UiAutomation
  connection apparently suppresses enabled accessibility services: with a session open,
  `adb shell dumpsys accessibility` reports "Bound services:{}" for both installed Gaten services, and
  the trigger broadcast is enqueued with 0 receivers; with the session closed the same dump reports the
  Gaten services bound and the trigger reaches the app. agent-device is used for everything it can do
  here without tearing down the surface under test (the record command and the broadcast itself); the
  tap and the stills come from adb. This limitation is the same one recorded in
  docs/pr-evidence/external-trigger/VERIFICATION.md.
